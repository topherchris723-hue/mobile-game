# Game Brief

The agents treat this file as the owner's word. The owner can edit it at any time.

## Core
- **Working title:** none yet. The game-designer proposes 5 original titles in round 1
  and the owner picks one.
- **Genre:** Anime fighting card battler (gacha collection)
- **Battle style:** Dragon Ball Legends-style, rebuilt in 2D:
  - Real-time 1v1 fights against AI opponents
  - Each player brings a team of 3 fighters and can switch between them during the fight
  - Attacks come from a hand of cards (e.g. melee, ranged, special, ultimate) that refills
    over time and costs energy
  - Swipe to move and dodge, tap cards to attack
  - Combos, counters and a team-wide finishing move as hype moments
  - All mechanic names, card names and terms must be our own, never Legends' terms
- **Setting / theme:** Martial arts / power-ups: fighters, transformations, energy blasts
- **Tone:** Hype and over-the-top: huge attacks, big shouts, epic moments
- **Inspirations (feel only, never copy):** Dragon Ball Legends (main), Dokkan Battle,
  One Piece Treasure Cruise

## First milestone
- **Scope:** Battle + summon screen
- **Number of characters at milestone:** 6–10 original fighters
- **Number of stages at milestone:** 3–5 story stages against AI

## Platform
- Android first, portrait orientation
- Offline only (fights against AI, no PvP) until the owner says otherwise
- No real-money purchases until the owner says otherwise

## Things I definitely want
- Fights that feel fast, flashy and powerful
- Transformations / power-ups as big moments

## Things I definitely don't want
- Any characters, names or designs copied from existing franchises
