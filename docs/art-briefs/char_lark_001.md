# Art brief: Lark, "Twin-Kite Courier" (`char_lark_001`)

Aura **Gale** (swirl, key #3DDC97) · Role **blaster** · Rarity **★3** · Ignition **none** (base form only)
Read first: `docs/STYLE_GUIDE.md` (§1, §4, §4.4, §4.5).

## Who
Fast-talking, always late, never misses. Delivers parcels across the peaks on wind-kite fins.
Expression default: cheeky smirk, eyes on the target. Attacks: one eye closed, aiming.

## Silhouette keys
1. **Two kite-shaped wind fins on the back**, diamond-shaped, rising above the shoulders like folded wings; they
   open sideways when she dashes or fires.
2. **Short rounded bob** (a clean dome over the head).
3. A **messenger bag** diagonal across the chest, bag on the back hip. Slim (36 px), 100 px tall, always mid-stride
   (one heel off the floor even in idle).

## Outfit
Short sky-blue zip jacket, dark leggings, light running shoes, fingerless gloves, chest-crossing messenger bag
with a parcel tag (blank, no text), kite fins mounted on a small harness between the shoulder blades.

## Pose sheet
| Pose | Description |
|---|---|
| idle | Weight on the back foot, front heel up, one hand on the bag strap, fins half-folded and twitching. |
| dash | Fins fully open sideways, body almost horizontal, gliding. |
| windup | Arm drawn back like throwing a parcel, wind gathers in the palm as a small swirl. Hold-able. |
| hit | Fins fold in, body crumples, bag swings up. |
| KO | Spins once and lands sitting, fins collapsed over her like a tent. |
| Ignition form | N/A in the milestone. Reserve for later: fins grow to 3 pairs. |
| victory | Hands a (blank) parcel to the camera, salutes with two fingers. |

## What makes it original
Courier + kite: the fins are paper-kite diamonds on a harness, not feathered wings, not a jetpack, not a glider
cape. Her attacks are "deliveries" (throws and wind lances).

## Must not resemble / avoid
- No broom, no red hair bow, no black cat companion, no witch dress (avoid the "flying delivery girl" classic).
- No feathered wings, no angel motif, no green tunic-and-cap archer look.
- No airbender arrow tattoos, no glider staff.
