# Art brief: Rook, "Thousand-Step Rook" (`char_rook_001`)

Aura **Gale** (swirl, key #3DDC97) · Role **striker** · Rarity **★4** · Ignition **Checkmate Gale**
Read first: `docs/STYLE_GUIDE.md` (§1, §4, §4.4, §4.5). Umbral variant: `Umbral Rook` (style guide §9).
He is the most frequent enemy (stages 1–3) and appears in mirror matches: his silhouette must read clearly from
both sides.

## Who
Cocky street champion who counts every hit out loud and calls fights like chess games.
Expression default: smug half-lidded grin. Attacks: counting, mouth open ("one! two!").

## Silhouette keys
1. **Lanky**: 108 px tall, only 36 px shoulders, long legs (legs are 45% of height).
2. **One hand always raised** beside the head with fingers up, counting (the other in the hoodie pocket in idle).
3. **Hood bunched behind the neck** and windswept hair swept forward over the brow.

## Pose sheet
| Pose | Description |
|---|---|
| idle | Hips cocked, one hand in pocket, the other raised counting on fingers (1-2-3 cycles over the loop), heels bouncing on the springs. |
| dash | Springs compress, he skims low, hood flapping. |
| windup | Steps back with one leg raised for a kick, fingers showing the count. Hold-able. |
| hit | Springs buckle, he flails, fingers splay. |
| KO | Spins and drops flat on his back, one finger still raised. |
| Ignition form | **Checker squares lift off the hoodie and orbit him as floating black/white tiles** (8–12 tiles) inside a Gale spiral; the hoodie becomes solid pale green #B8F2C8 with dark trims; spring soles glow Gale key. Back-shape: a rotating square-and-swirl pattern at ≤ 40% alpha. |
| ignition_transform | He snaps his fingers; the squares peel off his sleeves one by one and spin up. |
| victory | Taps his temple, then points five fingers at the camera: "Checkmate." |

## What makes it original
A chess-hustler street fighter: the counting gesture, checker pattern and spring-steel shoes are his own
vocabulary. His wind is "moves on a board", not ninja wind.

## Must not resemble / avoid
- No white spiky-haired kid with a skateboard and claw hands; no assassin-family look.
- No over-arched "dramatic menacing pose" with sound-effect letters; no chess piece on the head.
- No blue gi, no ninja headband, no long scarf.

## Mood references (words)

2. Street basketball at night under a single floodlight.
