# Art brief: Dunmore, "The Walking Wall" (`char_dunmore_001`)

Aura **Stone** (hexagon, key #C9A36B) · Role **guardian** · Rarity **★3** · Ignition **none** (base form only)
Read first: `docs/STYLE_GUIDE.md` (§1, §4, §4.4, §4.5).

## Who
Gentle giant, former quarry worker, apologises before every punch.
Expression default: worried kind smile, brows up. Attacks: eyes shut, apologetic grimace.

## Silhouette keys
1. **Wide square block**: 70 px shoulders on a 112 px body, the widest fighter by far. Short legs.
2. **Gauntlets of stacked river stones**: each forearm ends in a cluster of 3–4 round stones, fists bigger than his
   head.
3. **Braided beard** hanging to mid-chest; **tiny round spectacles** (two glints).

## What makes it original
A shy, apologetic quarry worker whose armour is river stones: soft personality in the biggest body. Earth power is
craft (stacked stones, chisel), not a monster or golem.

## Must not resemble / avoid
- No long tangled hair with a moleskin coat and umbrella (avoid the famous gentle-giant gamekeeper).
- No horned helmet, no axe, no braided-beard-dwarf-with-armour fantasy look.
- No rock-skinned body (he is a human wearing stones), no orange rock monster.
