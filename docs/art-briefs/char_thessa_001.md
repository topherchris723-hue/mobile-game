# Art brief: Thessa, "The Quiet Eclipse" (`char_thessa_001`)

Aura **Void** (crescent, key #9B6BFF) · Role **trickster** · Rarity **★4** · Ignition **Eclipse Unmasked**
Read first: `docs/STYLE_GUIDE.md` (§1, §4, §4.4, §4.5).

## Who
Dry, quiet, always watching. Corvane's herald, starting to doubt her king.
Expression default: flat, unimpressed, one visible eye narrowed. Attacks: no shout face, just a sharp look.

## Silhouette keys
1. **Ink-black long coat** to the shins, flaring out from the waist: a tall narrow triangle (44 px shoulders, 60 px
   hem, 110 px tall).
2. **Porcelain half-mask** over her left eye (the upper-left quarter of the face).
3. **Ink dripping from the fingertips**: 3–4 hanging drops under each hand.

## What makes it original
An ink-and-eclipse herald: her power is calligraphy ink and night sky, her mask is a repaired theatre mask
(gold-line repair = she is already "mended", a story hook for her doubt).

## Must not resemble / avoid
- **The mask is never bone-like, never skull-shaped, never toothed, never horned, never with red stripes.** It is a
  smooth porcelain theatre half-mask with one gold-repaired crack. (Avoid any "monster mask growing on the face"
  look; see the GDD §3 banned-term list for the related franchise term.)
- No black hooded cloak with red clouds; no plague-doctor beak; no katana.
- No glowing red eyes; her dark eyes have a violet ring.

## Mood references (words)
1. Ink dropped in water, spreading in slow clouds.
2. A theatre after closing: one spotlight, an empty stage, a mask on a chair.
3. A total solar eclipse: black disc, thin bright ring.
