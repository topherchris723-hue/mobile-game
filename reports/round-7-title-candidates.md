## 0. Brief and constraints applied

- Owner, round 7: "I want an original name for our game". Jade Tempest and the old shortlist are dropped.
- Compliance rule: a live trademark on any distinctive word of the title = FAIL. So each title has **one coined
  distinctive word**. It may be followed only by generic descriptive words ("Card Duels", "Arena", "Card Battle").
- Every candidate is under 20 characters, including the optional generic suffix (e.g. `Kovanta: Card Duels` = 19).
- §3 check (list read fresh from GDD §3 on 2026-11-02): no candidate contains a banned term. Checked against
  Arts, Ki, Rising, Vanish, Sparking, Spark/Sparks (name-only rule), Zenkai, Dragon, Ball, Saiyan, Dokkan, Awaken,
  Haki, Chakra, Jutsu, Bankai, Hollow, Quirk, Stand, Legends. Also checked against the §1 echo list (Dragon, Ball, Z,
  Legends, Saiyan, Piece, Treasure, Dokkan, Rising). No candidate starts with "Ki", "Kai" or "Zen" (Ki / Supreme Kai /
  Zenkai echoes). I also kept out Japanese "Ryu" (dragon) and "Geki" (echoes *Gekiranger*, a martial-arts Super Sentai show).
- I am not a lawyer. This is a web screen, not a trademark clearance (see §4, Limitations).
